## Federal TTB Forms

### TTB Form 5120.17 — Report of Wine Premises Operations
- **Status**: Not yet uploaded
- **Purpose**: Monthly report of all wine production activity
- **Key data needed from CellarTraks**: Beginning/ending inventory, production volumes, wine removed (sold/transferred), losses, wine in bond

### TTB Form 5110.40 — Monthly Report of Production Operations (Distillery)
- **Status**: Not yet uploaded
- **Purpose**: Monthly report of all distilled spirits production
- **Key data needed from CellarTraks**: Spirits produced (proof gallons), materials used, spirits in storage, spirits withdrawn

### TTB Form 5130.9 — Brewer's Report of Operations
- **Status**: Not yet uploaded
- **Purpose**: Monthly/quarterly report of all brewing operations
- **Key data needed from CellarTraks**: Beer produced (barrels), materials used, beer removed for consumption/sale
