## Federal TTB Forms

### TTB Form 5120.17 — Report of Wine Premises Operations
- **Status**: UPLOADED (February 2026)
- **Detailed reference**: `reference/ttb-f5120-17-wine-premises.md`
- **Purpose**: Monthly report of all wine production activity
- **Reporting unit**: Wine gallons
- **Filing**: Monthly (or quarterly/annual if qualified); due 15th after period end
- **Key sections**: Part I Section A (Bulk Wines by tax class), Part I Section B (Bottled Wines), Part III (Distilled Spirits in proof gallons), Part IV (Materials), Part VI (Distilling Material/Vinegar Stock), Part VII (Fermenters), Part VIII-IX (Special wines)
- **Tax class columns**: Not Over 16%, Over 16-21%, Over 21-24%, Artificially Carbonated, Sparkling (BF/BP), Hard Cider
- **Taxable events**: Section A Line 14 (bulk removed taxpaid), Section B Line 8 (bottled removed taxpaid)

### TTB Form 5110.40 — Monthly Report of Production Operations (Distillery)
- **Status**: UPLOADED (February 2026)
- **Detailed reference**: `reference/ttb-f5110-40-distillery-production.md`
- **Purpose**: Monthly report of all distilled spirits production
- **Reporting unit**: Proof gallons
- **Filing**: Monthly; due 15th after month end
- **Key sections**: Part I (Transactions by spirit type), Part II (Production of 190+ proof by material), Part III (Whisky by kind/cooperage), Part IV (Brandy by kind), Part V (Redistillation), Part VI (Materials used)
- **Spirit columns**: Whisky (160 and under / over 160), Brandy (170 and under / over 170), Rum, Gin, Vodka, Alcohol 190+, Alcohol under 190, Other
- **Also covers**: TTB F 5110.11 (Storage Operations) and TTB F 5110.28 (Processing Operations)

### TTB Form 5130.9 — Brewer's Report of Operations
- **Status**: UPLOADED (February 2026)
- **Detailed reference**: `reference/ttb-f5130-9-brewers-report.md`
- **Purpose**: Monthly/quarterly report of all brewing operations
- **Reporting unit**: Barrels (31 gallons), rounded to 2 decimal places
- **Filing**: Monthly if >$50K tax liability, Quarterly if ≤$50K; due 15th day after period end
- **Key sections**: Part 1 (Beer Summary across 5 location columns), Part 2 (Cereal Beverage), Part 3 (Remarks)
- **Location columns**: Cellar, Racking-Bulk, Racking-Keg, Bottling-Bulk, Bottling-Case
- **Taxable lines**: Line 14 (removed for sale) and Line 15 (removed to tavern)

## Massachusetts State Forms

### Form AB-1 — Alcoholic Beverages Excise Return
- **Status**: UPLOADED (February 2026)
- **Detailed reference**: `reference/ma-form-ab1-excise-return.md`
- **Purpose**: Monthly excise tax return for MA — calculate and pay state excise tax
- **Filing**: Monthly; due 20th day of month following reporting month
- **Main page**: 9 tax lines computing excise by beverage category
- **Schedules**: A (Malt), B1 (Still Wine), B2 (Sparkling), C (≤15% ABV), D (>15-50% ABV), E (>50% ABV), F (Deductions), G (Alcohol), I (Cider)
- **Tax method**: Inventory-based "gallons sold" for most categories; "gallons acquired" for malt
- **Maps to CellarTraks state tax classes**: malt_beverages, still_wine, sparkling_wine, alcoholic_beverages_15_or_less, distilled_spirits_15_to_50, distilled_spirits_over_50, hard_cider
