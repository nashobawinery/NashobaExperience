# TTB Form 5120.17 — Report of Wine Premises Operations

**Form Number**: TTB F 5120.17sm (01/2018)
**OMB Number**: 1513-0053
**Agency**: Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (TTB), Department of the Treasury
**Uploaded**: February 2026

## Overview

This is the primary federal report for winery operations. It tracks all wine inventory — production, receipts, removals, losses, spirits usage, and materials — for a reporting period. It is the most complex of the three division reports.

## Filing Requirements

| Condition | Frequency | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Monthly | 15th day after end of month |
| Qualifies under 27 CFR 24.300(g)(2) | Quarterly or Annual | 15th day after end of period |
| No reportable operations expected | May skip months (note in Part X) | Resume when operations occur |

- Prepare in duplicate; send original to TTB National Revenue Center (Cincinnati)
- Keep copy on bonded wine premises for TTB inspection
- Can file Original, Amended, or Final Report

## Reporting Unit

**All wine quantities reported in GALLONS (wine gallons)**

## Part I — Summary of Wines in Bond (Gallons)

This is the core of the report with two sections: Bulk Wines and Bottled Wines.

### Column Structure (Wine Tax Classes by ABV)

| Column | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Not Over 16 Percent | Still wine ≤16% ABV |
| (b) | Over 16 to 21 Percent (Inclusive) | Fortified/higher ABV still wine |
| (c) | Over 21 to 24 Percent (Inclusive) | High ABV wine (rare) |
| (d) | Artificially Carbonated Wine | Wine with added CO2 |
| (e) | Sparkling Wine | Sub-divided into BF (Bottle Fermented) and BP (Bulk Process) |
| (f) | Hard Cider | Apple/pear-derived, 0.5% to <8.5% ABV |

### Section A — Bulk Wines (Lines 1-32)

#### Additions (Lines 1-12)

| Line | Description | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | On hand beginning of period | Carry forward from line 31 of last report |
| 2 | Produced by fermentation | Volume measured when fermentation complete or removed from fermenter. Col (e): BF = bottle fermented sparkling, BP = bulk process sparkling |
| 3 | Produced by sweetening | Volume AFTER sweetening materials added (before amount shown on line 18) |
| 4 | Produced by addition of wine spirits | Volume AFTER spirits added; verify ABV and place in correct column |
| 5 | Produced by blending | Volume AFTER blending wines of different tax classes (before amount on line 20) |
| 6 | Produced by amelioration | Volume AFTER ameliorating materials added |
| 7 | Received in bond | Untaxpaid bulk wine received from another bonded winery/cellar |
| 8 | Bottled wine dumped to bulk | Bottled wine emptied back into bulk account (same amount as Section B line 10) |
| 9 | Inventory gains | Positive variance discovered during complete physical inventory |
| 10 | Write-in: Change of Tax Class | Move wine between columns when ABV reclassified, or sparkling returned to still |
| 11 | Write-in: Formula Wine Produced | Volume of formula wine AFTER adding flavors/materials to base |
| 12 | **TOTAL** | Sum of lines 1-11 |

#### Removals (Lines 13-32)

| Line | Description | Tax Implications |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Bottled | Wine bottled during period (must match Section B line 2). Col (e): BF/BP split |
| 14 | Removed taxpaid | Bulk wine taxably removed | **TAXABLE** |
| 15 | Transfers in bond | Untaxpaid bulk wine shipped to another bonded winery/cellar |
| 16 | Removed for distilling material | Sent to distilled spirits plant (also show in Part VI) |
| 17 | Removed to vinegar plant | Sent to vinegar plant (also show in Part VI) |
| 18 | Used for sweetening | Amount BEFORE sweetening (after amount shown on line 3) |
| 19 | Used for addition of wine spirits | Amount BEFORE spirits added (after amount on line 4) |
| 20 | Used for blending | Amount BEFORE blending different tax classes (after amount on line 5). Only report if different tax classes blended. |
| 21 | Used for amelioration | Amount BEFORE amelioration |
| 22 | Used for effervescent wine | Base wine used for sparkling production |
| 23 | Used for testing | Lab samples |
| 24-28 | Write-in entries | Change of Tax Class (decrease side), Formula Wine Used, Removed to DM/VS Account, Destroyed (with TTB permission) |
| 29 | Losses (other than inventory) | Casualty losses (spillage, equipment failure) — must report to TTB |
| 30 | Inventory losses | Negative variance from physical inventory — must explain in Part X |
| 31 | **On hand end of period** | Line 12 minus lines 13-30 |
| 32 | **TOTAL** | Must equal line 12 |

## What CellarTraks Must Track (Summary)

### Bulk Wine Inventory by Tax Class
- Not Over 16% ABV
- Over 16% to 21% ABV
- Over 21% to 24% ABV
- Artificially Carbonated
- Sparkling (Bottle Fermented vs. Bulk Process)
- Hard Cider

### Production Events
- Fermentation completions (volume, ABV determination, tax class assignment)
- Sweetening operations (before/after volumes)
- Wine spirits additions (before/after volumes, ABV verification)
- Blending operations (only cross-tax-class blends, before/after)
- Amelioration operations
- Formula wine production

### Wine Movement
- Receipts in bond from other premises
- Transfers in bond to other premises
- Bottling operations (bulk → bottled, with BF/BP split for sparkling)
- Dumping bottled wine back to bulk
- Removals for distilling material or vinegar stock
- Taxpaid removals (taxable events)
- Export removals
- Tasting/testing usage
- Family use removals

### Materials Tracking
- Raw grape materials (pounds uncrushed, gallons crushed)
- Juice and concentrate (gallons)
- Other fruit materials (specify kind)
- Sugar (dry pounds, liquid gallons)
- Distilled spirits received and used (proof gallons by type)

### Inventory Management
- Beginning/ending inventory for bulk AND bottled wine by tax class
- Physical inventory reconciliation (gains/losses)
- Fermenter contents at period end
- Distilling material and vinegar stock on hand

### Loss/Shortage Tracking
- Casualty losses with explanations
- Inventory variances with explanations
- Bottled wine breakage
