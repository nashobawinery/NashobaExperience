# TTB Form 5110.40 — Monthly Report of Production Operations (Distillery)

**Form Number**: TTB F 5110.40 (09/2025)
**OMB Number**: 1513-0047
**Agency**: Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (TTB), Department of the Treasury
**Uploaded**: February 2026

## Overview

This is the primary federal report for distillery PRODUCTION operations. It tracks spirits produced, materials used, and how produced spirits were disposed of. Note: This covers production only — storage operations are tracked separately on TTB F 5110.11.

## Related Forms Also Uploaded

| Form | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **TTB F 5110.40** (this form) | Monthly Report of Production Operations |
| **TTB F 5110.11** | Monthly Report of Storage Operations |
| **TTB F 5110.28** | Monthly Report of Processing Operations |

## Filing Requirements

- **Frequency**: Monthly (all DSP proprietors)
- **Due Date**: 15th day of month following the reporting period
- **Submit to**: Director, National Revenue Center, Cincinnati OH
- **Prepare**: In duplicate; retain copy on premises

## Reporting Unit

**All spirits quantities reported in PROOF GALLONS**
(1 proof gallon = 1 gallon at 100 proof / 50% ABV)

## What CellarTraks Must Track (Summary)

### Spirits Production
- Total proof gallons produced by spirit type (whisky, brandy, rum, gin, vodka, alcohol 190+, other)
- Whisky breakdown by kind (bourbon, corn, rye, light) and cooperage type (new, used, tank)
- Brandy breakdown by kind (grape, other, neutral)
- Material source for high-proof spirits (grain, fruit, molasses, etc.)

### Spirit Dispositions
- Tax-paid withdrawals (taxable)
- Entries to storage, processing, and bond transfer accounts
- Withdrawals for wine production, export, R&D, government use
- Redistillation receipts

### Raw Materials Consumed
- Grain by type (corn, rye, malt, wheat, sorghum, barley) in pounds and gallons
- Fruit by type in pounds and gallons
- Cane products (molasses) in pounds and gallons
- Other materials

### Inventory
- Quarterly physical inventory of redistillation stock and unfinished spirits

# TTB Form 5110.28 — Monthly Report of Processing Operations

**Form Number**: TTB F 5110.28 (03/2016)
**OMB Number**: 1513-0041

## Overview

Tracks spirits in the PROCESSING account — where bulk spirits are bottled, packaged, blended, and prepared for market.

## Parts

### Part I — Bulk Ingredients (Proof Gallons)

Two columns: Wine and Spirits

| Line | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | On hand first of month |
| 2 | Received (other than line 3) |
| 3 | Alcohol for fuel use received from customs |
| 4 | Alcohol, flavor, materials dumped |
| 5 | Wine mixed with spirits |
| 6 | Dumped for further processing |
| 7 | Gains |
| 8 | **TOTAL** |
| 9-20 | Various withdrawals and dispositions |
| 24 | Losses |
| 25 | On hand end of month |
| 26 | **TOTAL** (must equal line 8) |

### Part II — Finished Products (Proof Gallons)

Two columns: Bottled and Packaged

| Line | Description |
|---|---|
| 27 | On hand first of month |
| 28 | Bottled or packaged |
| 29 | Received |
| 30 | Inventory overages |
| 31 | **TOTAL** |
| 32-45 | Various withdrawals and dispositions |
| 46 | On hand end of month |
| 47 | **TOTAL** (must equal line 31) |

### Part IV — Processing of Beverage Spirits (Detailed Breakdown)

Detailed grid tracking each spirit KIND through processing:
- Bulk spirits dumped into processing
- Bottled (imported, domestic, bottled in bond, for export)
- Covers: Alcohol/Neutral Spirits, Blended Whiskey varieties, Imported Whiskey (Scotch/Canadian/Irish), Domestic Whiskey, Brandy, Rum, Gin, Vodka, Cordials/Liqueurs, Cocktails/Mixed Drinks, Tequila

### What CellarTraks Must Track for Processing

- Bulk ingredients received and on hand (wine and spirits in proof gallons)
- Bottling/packaging operations by spirit kind
- Finished product inventory
- All withdrawals (taxpaid, export, destroyed, etc.)
