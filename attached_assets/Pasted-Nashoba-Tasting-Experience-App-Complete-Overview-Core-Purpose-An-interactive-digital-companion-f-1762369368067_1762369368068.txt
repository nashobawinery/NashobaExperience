Nashoba Tasting Experience App - Complete Overview
Core Purpose
An interactive digital companion for tasting room guests that educates, entertains, personalizes recommendations, and facilitates purchases while collecting valuable feedback.

Guest Experience Flow
Welcome & Name Entry - Guest enters name, creating their unique session with aerial winery video background
Optional Preference Quiz - 3-question quiz for immediate personalized recommendations (can skip)
Interactive Browsing - Explore products by category, view details, add favorites with notes, build cart
Trivia Challenges - Fun fact popups appear periodically (configurable timing)
AI Recommendations - After 2+ interactions, AI suggests 4-6 personalized products
Tasting Complete & Checkout - Review favorites, add to cart, email notes and order
Feedback Survey - Post-tasting survey to improve experience

🧠 AI Recommendation Engine
How It Works:

Data Collection - Tracks favorites, views, notes, cart additions
Profile Building - Creates taste profile (wine characteristics, types, flavors, patterns)
AI Analysis - Sends to language model that analyzes like a sommelier
Personalized Display - Shows 4-6 "AI Picks" with explanations
Requirements:

Activates after 2+ interactions
Auto-generates when data available
Guest can manually refresh anytime
🎯 Interactive Trivia System
Timing: Popups at configurable intervals (default: first after 2 min, then every 4 min)
Format: Multiple choice with 4 answer options
Feedback: Immediate feedback with explanation
Progress Tracking: Score card shows progress
No Repeats: Won't show same question twice
10 Question Cap: Stops after 10 questions
Perfect Score Reward:

10/10 correct = $5 credit applied to cart
Celebration screen with confetti
One-time reward per session

Best Practices for Staff
Introducing the App:

"Welcome! Use our digital companion on your phone - like having a personal sommelier!"
Highlight: favorites with notes, trivia for $5 credit, AI recommendations, cart building
During Tasting:

Let app enhance, not replace your interaction
Encourage note-taking: "You'll get these emailed later!"
Highlight AI picks: "Check your recommendations - they're eerily accurate!"
Gamify: "10 right answers = $5 off!"
Upsell naturally: "Add one more bottle for 10% off instead of 5%!"
At Checkout:

Orders sent to onsiteorder@nashobawinery.com
Verify discounts and trivia credits applied
Fulfill items from email
Confirm guest received email confirmation
Product Management:

Mark sold out immediately
Update prices when they change
Use badges for seasonal items, new arrivals, staff favorites
Import from Shopify weekly
Add high-quality photos
