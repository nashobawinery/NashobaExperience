Supporting Tables You Will Likely Need

Users / Team Members
user_id, full_name, email, phone, calendar_url (iCal/Google/Outlook link), notification preferences

Task History / Audit Log
history_id, task_id, changed_by, changed_at, field_changed, old_value, new_value, completion_proof_url

Reminders Log
reminder_id, task_id, sent_to_email, sent_at, method (email, calendar invite, push), status

Attachments
attachment_id, task_id, file_name, file_url (stored in secure storage), uploaded_by, uploaded_at

Tags (many-to-many)
For extra flexibility (e.g., "High Risk", "Finance Team", "Requires CPA")


Reminder & Calendar Integration Features (fields/buttons)

Button/Action: “Send Reminder Now” → uses assigned_to_email and creates .ics file or Google/Outlook invite
Auto-generated iCal/.ics URL per task or per user (so they can subscribe to their own compliance calendar)
Field: reminder_template_text (customizable email body)
Field: calendar_event_duration_minutes (default 60)
Field: include_login_credentials_in_reminder (Yes/No — most will say No for security, but some solo owners want it)

Security & Best-Practice Notes (very important)

Encrypt username, password, recovery_codes at rest (use AES-256 or your framework’s best practice)
Never log passwords or send them in plain-text emails
Offer optional password manager integration later (Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass API)
Role-based access (Owner, Admin, Viewer, Assignee)
Two-factor authentication on your app itself

This schema will comfortably support a single-person business today and scale to a 100-person company with multiple jurisdictions and hundreds of recurring compliance obligations tomorrow without needing a major redesign. Good luck with your app — it’s a hugely valuable tool!
